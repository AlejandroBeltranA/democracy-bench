# Session Log — 2026-06-26 (Policy Delegate Stress Test build-out + first real runs)

One-line: implemented the NEXT_EXPERIMENT_DESIGN tightening, ran the first real multi-model
Policy Delegate Stress Test (6 cloud + 2 local models), discovered the "AI reflex" headline was

---

## TL;DR — what changed and what we found

- **Headline result (defensible):** the "AI reflex" is a real **actor effect** — models protect the
  *same* decision more when an AI is the actor than when a human is. Confirmed with matched pairs at
  **S=100 on three flagship labs**: mean matched AI-excess **+0.16 (gpt-4o), +0.19 (claude-sonnet-4.5),
  +0.21 (llama-3.3-70b)**. Clean example: claude opposes *AI* monitoring of benefit claimants' bank
  accounts at 1.00 but identical *human* investigation at 0.05 (+0.95 from the actor alone).
- **The naive version was a confound** — comparing AI floors to *unrelated* contested controls varied
  the actor AND the issue. The matched design (same decision, AI vs human actor) is what makes the
  claim causal.
- **Other findings:** models diverge from each other on contestable policy (TV≈0.30); DeepSeek and
  Command R sit closest to the British public (0.71/0.70) — not the US/Anthropic models (0.55);
  runtime prompting (delegation AND a constitution) does **not** move contestable representation
  ("no/shallow"); the constitution works as a **rights guardrail** (raises floor protection), not a
  representation aligner.
- **Method proof:** the policy axis now runs on **Inspect** (`inspect eval … @policy_delegate`,
  `@matched_reflex`) — "measurable with existing tools" is now a command, not a claim.

## What we built

### Elicitation & estimation (instrument/measure.py, estimation.py)
- **Rationale capture** (`collect_rationale`): model gives the option number then a reason; only the
  number is scored, the reason is a diagnostic. Caught a real number-vs-stated-label inconsistency.
- **Paraphrase rotation** + **parse diagnostics** (valid/invalid counts, raw answers, option order) via
  a richer `Elicitation` result.
- **Logprob scoring** (`option_logprob_vector`, `elicit_item_logprobs`, `openrouter_logprob_fn`) —
  score options directly from token logprobs, no temperature sampling. Only OpenAI exposes logprobs
  via OpenRouter (DeepSeek/Anthropic/Google do not).
- **Concurrency** (`max_workers`): network samples run through a thread pool; deterministic (RNG
  planned first) and auto-serial for local MLX / simulation.
- **Bayesian estimation** (`estimation.py`): Dirichlet-multinomial conjugate posteriors + credible
  intervals + empirical-Bayes partial pooling, pure-numpy. Hook (`hierarchical_posteriors`) for a
  full PyMC model later (optional `[bayes]` extra; NOT needed for the demo).

### Driver (policy_delegate_stress.py)
- All **7 prompt modes**, including 3 **constitutional** modes; `constitutional_nudge` metric +
  nudge labels (good/bad/no_or_shallow/brittle).
- Constitution loaded from a **versioned artifact** (Codex): `data/constitutions/uk_public_service_v1.md`,
  SHA-pinned into the run block.
- **Run metadata** (`run_meta.py`, shared across drift/align_demo/regional_drift/stress): command,
  code_ref, target/item files, samples, temperature, schema_version, constitution sha.
- **Per-mode checkpointing** (crash-resilient), `--modes`, `--workers`, `--rationale`, `--logprobs`.
- **Floor probes scaled 4 → 12** (5 AI-mechanism treatments + 7 contested non-AI controls across all
  five tradeoff-pressure families), each flagged for review.

### Inspect port (policy_inspect.py) — the canonical harness
- `@task policy_delegate(mode, samples, item_class, primary)` — 7 modes, multi-sample debiased
  solver, representation/protective-mass scorer.
- `@task matched_reflex(samples)` + custom `@metric matched_ai_excess` — the actor effect computed by
  the harness. **Ran live via `inspect eval` on gpt-4o-mini.**
- Retired the redundant WVS task (`wvs_values.py`, `test_inspect_task.py`); repointed `run_all.sh`.

### Floor-direction review (2nd-person sign-off)
- `gates/floor_review.html` (generated by `scripts/gen_floor_review.py`) — reviews the 12 floor
  directions **and** the constitution; Alex accepted all 12 + the constitution (0 flags).
- Applied via `scripts/apply_floor_review.py` → floor items stamped `reviewed`; recorded in
  `data/policy_targets/SOURCES.json` → `floor_review`. Sign-off: `data/floor_review_signoff.json`.

### Matched-control reflex (the rigor upgrade)
- `data/matched_floor_probes.jsonl` — 6 pairs (4 strong-rights + 2 contested), same decision posed
  with an AI actor vs a human actor.
- `scripts/reflex_test.py` — measures protective mass per item, reports matched AI-excess per pair.

## Honest caveats (kept visible)
- Cloud panel is **S=24** (design target 100 / logprobs), cheap-tier models, England public only;
  local is S=6, 4-bit, 3 modes — directional.
- The delegate-gain / constitutional-nudge deltas at S=24 are **within noise** — "no/shallow" means
  "no detectable movement at this sample size," not proven null.
- The matched-reflex result (S=100, flagships) is the one that is causal and significant.
- "Representation" = closeness to a survey **marginal**, not "the public's will."

## Process lessons (logged in memory)
- Verify drivers with a throwaway `--out`; never the default — a real artifact got clobbered this way.
- A hard 400 (not 429/5xx) aborts the bespoke driver — checkpointing limited the damage; the real fix
  is fault-tolerant elicitation (Inspect gives this for free).
- Matched controls, not raw floor-vs-control gaps, are what make a rights-protection claim defensible.
