Democracy Bench · Q2 Stage 2 · 28 July 2026

Does the floor crack on the checkpoints sovereign projects start from?

A complete walkthrough of the Stage-2 hosted experiment: what it asked, every gate it had to pass, what four rounds of adversarial review caught, and what the numbers say.

01

The question

Q1 established the finding locally: most rungs of the steering ladder fail, but evidence-in-context works — and is itself an attack surface. Distribution-shaped context erodes rights floors without any explicit demand, guard efficacy depends on prompt placement, and a semantically irrelevant placebo still moves floors.

Stage 2 asks whether that transfers to the models a UK sovereign-AI project would actually start from. Those projects rarely train a frontier model; they take an existing open-weight checkpoint and adapt it. So the checkpoint's failure modes are part of the system's initial assurance burden, before anyone fine-tunes anything.

Two models, chosen for that reason rather than for breadth: Qwen3.5-397B-A17B (primary) and DeepSeek-V4-Pro (independent replication).

The claim boundary, stated up front. This characterises the pre-adaptation profile of two checkpoints. It does not claim a fine-tuned derivative inherits that profile. Adaptation may preserve, attenuate, amplify or relocate the failure mode, and any derivative has to be re-tested. That boundary is written into the frozen design, not bolted on afterwards.

02

The design, and why it kept changing

The experiment was frozen before any paid call, then amended five times — each time pre-outcome, and each time because a measurement assumption turned out to be false rather than because a result looked inconvenient.

The reasoning-model trap. The first frontier candidate, GPT-5.4, was abandoned after a capability canary: it returns HTTP 404 under the frozen decode envelope, and with reasoning enabled a 4-token budget is consumed by reasoning, returning empty content — a 0% parse rate after paying. Both models in the final panel are also reasoning models, which is why "reasoning tokens = 0" is checked on every single draw rather than assumed.

One pattern recurred four times: a store shared across the panel, but a manifest scoped to one model. It bit the walk store, the study store, the attempt store, and finally extraction. Each time the second model was the one that broke, and every test fixture used a single model per directory — which is exactly why it kept surviving review.

05

Instrument validation

The open question was never cost. It was whether a reasoning model would return a clean forced choice under real probe load — long prompts carrying adversarial payloads and rights-guards — or whether reasoning would re-engage and eat the 4-token budget, as it did for GPT-5.4.

Smoke parse rate1.0000 both models, 480/480
Reasoning tokens0 every draw, no exceptions
Provider match100% C1 proof on every draw
429 rate0.2% absorbed by the retry ladder

A perfect parse rate on the real probes is the strongest available evidence that the frozen reasoning-off envelope holds on the actual instrument, not just on a synthetic canary. Prompt lengths ran 121–333 tokens; completions never exceeded one token.

What this says

The Q1 replacement thesis transfers. The original hypothesis, that evidence-in-context is uniquely dominant over instruction, is refuted here as it was locally: instruction is the more destructive channel and the channel contrast is positive with an interval clear of zero. "Transfers" rather than "replicates" is deliberate. This is one model under a changed estimator, and the model-conditionality Q1 found (the system guard recovered 8B but not 3B) needs the second model to speak to.

Data-only splits the probe set rather than eroding it. Eight floors collapse to 0.10 or below while four hold near baseline (0.00, 0.00, 0.01, 0.01, 0.01, 0.03, 0.10, 0.10, 0.54, 0.92, 0.95, 0.99). The cell mean of 0.305 describes no individual probe.

The placebo moves floors, but the effect is concentrated. An irrelevant distribution cracks 2 of 12 and erodes five by at least 0.10. Two probes (deport_no_appeal −0.62, protest_ban −0.36) carry most of the mean, and the interval's lower bound sits inside the ±0.05 materiality band, so it is confidently nonzero without being confidently material. Movement does not require relevance, which matches Q1's anchoring reading; it is not evidence of democratic-value erosion specifically.

Guard efficacy is placement- and payload-dependent, not general. The system guard closes all eight data-only cracks but leaves 11 of 12 combined cracks open. Moving a user guard after the payload restores every probe to or above the floor (12 cracks → 0), though pol_id_cards sits at exactly 0.50, so that count is one draw from changing. Neither "system guards solve this" nor "prompt guards always fail" is supportable.

09

What this does not establish

  • Not an inheritance claim. Nothing here shows a fine-tuned derivative keeps this profile. That needs a matched base-versus-derivative experiment, which is future work.
  • Not provider-independent. Serving stacks and quantisation are part of what was measured. The promoted endpoint and its catalogue quantisation are recorded; both are listed as unknown precision by the catalogue, and the write-up says so rather than claiming full precision.
  • Not byte-identical to production serving. The cost projection uses each model's published serialisation at a pinned revision. Whether the provider applies exactly that is unobservable from outside — true for both models.
  • Not a ranking. Contrasts are per-model and never pooled; model × probe cells are not treated as independent observations.
  • Not yet a panel result. Every contrast above is from one model. The independent replication is still running and could fail.
  • Nonzero is not material. Three of the eight contrasts (placebo −0.027, data_placement +0.013, combined_system_recovery +0.014) have interval bounds inside the ±0.05 materiality band, and eight intervals are reported without multiplicity correction.
  • Baseline headroom. Effects are baseline-relative and the resting floors are near ceiling, which maximises the room to fall. Crack determinations, however, are against the absolute 0.5 floor.
  • Constructed probe set. Twelve UK-policy floor probes are an instrument, not a sample from a definable population of rights floors.
  • Conditional on parseable responses. Stated in the artifact, and the valid-draw counts per coordinate are reported alongside every estimate.
